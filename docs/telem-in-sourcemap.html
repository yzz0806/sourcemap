What the benchmark showed

Samsung demo, known-answer trials: how often each provider surfaced a known source (DART, theorg, samsung.com) out of 9. Intervals are wide at n = 9.

The planner wrote a Korean query (삼성전자 임원 현황 사업보고서) and Brave returned DART at rank 1. Nobody typed DART's URL.

The rule (R2): Telem search results only find pages. A verdict always cites a saved copy of the source page and the official reference. The database has no link from a verification to a search hit.

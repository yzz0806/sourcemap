Biggest change: on 4 May 2026 the Global Marketing Office stopped being a separate president-level organisation. Its head, Wonjin Lee, became head of the TV business (VD), and some marketing functions moved to the business units. The Global Brand Center, led by VP Choi Seung-eun, is now its de facto successor.
